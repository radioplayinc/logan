Privacy Policy

Logan ("Logan", "we", "us") is a Perfectdio product. It operates logan-a2b.pages.dev. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what choices you have. It covers only this website. It does not cover Google, Cloudflare, or any other site linked from this site.

Contact: Privacy questions and deletion requests go to info@radioplayinc.com.

Important: this site is an investor demo, not a live logistics service yet. The Dispatch and Drivers dashboards show fictional loads, drivers, trucks, messages, invoices, and payouts. No real freight is booked, tracked, or paid here.

1. What we collect

Nothing you type is sent to us. This site has no account signup, no mailing list form, and no contact form. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, address, payment details, or date of birth.

Demo entries stay in your browser only. When you create a load, send a demo message, or accept a delivery in the demo, that entry is held in your browser session only. It is not sent to a server, not stored in local storage, and not saved after you close or reload the page. Use Reset Demo to clear the demo back to its starting fictional dataset.

Technical information from our service providers. This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser information when your browser loads the site, as is normal for any website. We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any advertising tracker on this site as of the date above.

We do not collect precise location, health information, or financial information. The live map in the demo is a simulation drawn from fictional load data, not your location.

2. How we use information

We do not receive personal information from this site, so there is nothing for us to use, sell, or share. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not rent a mailing list, because there is no mailing list on this site.

3. Who receives your information

  • Cloudflare. Cloudflare hosts this site and may process technical logs to deliver and protect it.
  • Google Fonts. Google serves the Archivo font files for this site and may receive technical information when your browser loads them.
  • Legal requirements. We will disclose information only if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of Logan and its visitors.

4. How long we keep it

Demo entries you type are kept only for your browser session and are gone when you close or reload the page. We hold no personal information from this site to retain or delete. If that changes, for example if we add accounts, a mailing list, or a contact form, this policy will be updated first.

5. Your choices and rights

Because we collect no personal information on this site, there is no account to access, correct, or delete here. If you emailed us at info@radioplayinc.com, you can ask what we hold from that email, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws may have additional rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by those laws. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

6. Cookies

We do not set marketing or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for the limited technical cookies our hosting and font providers may set.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child sent us personal information, email info@radioplayinc.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

This site uses HTTPS. No system is completely secure, so do not send sensitive information to us through this demo. The demo message thread is fictional and local to your browser, and it is not a way to reach us. To contact us, email info@radioplayinc.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If we add accounts, analytics, advertising, a mailing list, a contact form, real dispatch, or any new way of collecting information, we will update this policy before that change goes live, and update the date at the top.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: info@radioplayinc.com. No business mailing address is published on this site yet. That is a gap to fill before the service launches.
