Refunds and Payments

No payment is taken on this site today, so there is nothing to refund or ship yet. Logan is an investor demo. Invoices and payouts shown in the Dispatch and Drivers dashboards are simulated, using fictional loads and a demo rule that driver pay is 30 percent of the load rate. No real money moves, no card is charged, and no payout is sent.

1. When real payments launch

Before any real payment can be taken or sent, this page will be updated with the full terms, including:

  • What is charged, to whom, and when, including any fees, taxes, and payout timing.
  • How refunds are requested and how long they take.
  • Whether refunds go back to the original payment method.
  • What happens if a load is cancelled, delayed, or not delivered.
  • How payout disputes are handled.
  • Who to contact and how long a response takes.

2. Demo invoices and payouts

Demo invoices and payouts are fictional records for the demo only. They are not bills, not payment promises, and not proof of any transaction. Demo entries stay in your browser session only and are gone when you close or reload the page.

3. No shipping

Logan does not ship physical goods. When the real service launches, delivery terms for freight will be stated in the service agreement before any load is booked, not only on this page.

4. Contact

Payment questions, once real payments launch: info@radioplayinc.com. Also see our Terms of Service.
